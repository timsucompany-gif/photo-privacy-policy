一、總覽

照片整理大師（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）由 TimSuCompany 開發與維護。我們非常重視您的隱私，本政策說明本應用程式如何處理您的資料。

核心承諾：本應用程式的所有照片與影片分析皆在您的裝置上本機執行，不會將您的照片或影片上傳至任何伺服器。

二、我們存取的資料

為了提供清理功能，本應用程式會存取以下裝置資料：

  • 照片與影片：讀取您裝置中的照片和影片，以進行模糊偵測、相似分組等分析。所有分析皆在裝置端完成。
  • 儲存空間：在 Android 9（API 28）及更早版本中，需要寫入權限以執行刪除操作。
  • 應用程式使用統計：用於計算各應用程式佔用的快取空間大小。

三、裝置端 AI 分析

本應用程式使用以下技術來分析您的照片與影片，所有處理皆在裝置端完成，不會傳送任何資料至外部：

  • 模糊偵測：使用 TensorFlow Lite 模型判斷照片是否模糊。
  • 人臉偵測：使用 Google ML Kit 偵測閉眼等情況。
  • 影片分析：使用感知雜湊演算法比對相似影片。
上述模型與演算法完全在您的手機上運行，無需網路連線即可使用核心功能。

本應用程式使用 Google AdMob 顯示第三方廣告。為提供、衡量及改善廣告服務，AdMob 可能會收集或處理與廣告及應用程式使用相關的資料。

1. Overview

Photo Organizer Master (the "App") is developed and maintained by TimSuCompany. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Core commitment: All photo and video analysis is performed locally on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to any server.

2. Data We Access

To provide cleanup features, the App accesses the following device data:

  • Photos & Videos: Read your device's photos and videos for blur detection, similarity grouping, and other analysis. All processing is done on-device.
  • Storage: On Android 9 (API 28) and earlier, write permission is required for deletion operations.
  • App Usage Stats: Used to calculate cache storage occupied by each app.

3. On-Device AI Analysis

The App uses the following technologies to analyze your photos and videos. All processing is performed entirely on your device — no data is sent externally:

  • Blur Detection: TensorFlow Lite models to determine if a photo is blurry.
  • Face Detection: Google ML Kit to detect closed eyes and similar conditions.
  • Video Analysis: Perceptual hashing algorithms to identify similar videos.
These models and algorithms run entirely on your phone. No internet connection is needed for core features.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display third-party advertisements. To provide, measure, and improve advertising services, AdMob may collect or process information related to advertising and app usage.

  • Device Identifiers: Such as advertising identifiers or other identifiers related to the device and advertising services.
  • Usage Data: Such as app interactions, advertising interactions, and related usage information.
  • Network and Device Information: Such as IP address and other technical information required to provide advertising services.
  • General Location Information: Non-precise location information may be used depending on the third-party advertising service and device settings.

Advertising purposes: The above information may be used by Google AdMob to provide, measure, improve, or personalize third-party advertisements. Actual data processing may vary depending on the platform, region, user settings, and AdMob configuration.

Important: The App itself does not upload your photos or videos to our own servers and does not intentionally provide your photos or videos to Google AdMob for advertising purposes. AdMob's data processing is governed by Google's services and privacy policies.

The collection and handling of such data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising Identifiers and Tracking

Because the App uses Google AdMob to display third-party advertisements, advertising services may use device identifiers and app usage data to provide, measure, improve, or personalize advertisements.

On iOS, if the advertising functionality involves tracking governed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, the App will request authorization through Apple's system. You may choose to allow or deny this permission.

Denying ATT authorization does not affect the App's core photo organization features, but it may affect ad personalization or advertising measurement.

On Android, advertising services may use the Android Advertising ID or other applicable advertising identifiers. Actual processing depends on Google AdMob and device settings.

5. Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Scan Cache: Analysis results for photos and videos to speed up subsequent scans.
  • Preferences: Your app settings.

This data is stored only on your device and can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not create user accounts or profiles
